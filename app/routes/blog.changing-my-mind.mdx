One of my favorite things about working in AI is how often I change my mind now.

In the before times, things moved slowly enough that you could have an opinion for months or years.

- [X] is the best JavaScript framework
- [Y] is the best way to test your app

Now, things move so fast that I find myself doing complete 180s every few weeks.

At one point, I couldn't imagine using a coding agent in anything but a terminal UI. OpenAI seemed to have an insurmountable lead. The idea of an automated software factory seemed ludicrous.

Then GUIs got better and I forced myself to try one, and immediately got much more productive. Anthropic has a big lead now with Opus 5.5. Software factories are starting to seem... less ludicrous.

Give me a good reason and I'll turn on a dime.

I used to think flip flopping was a bad thing, but the rapid pace of AI is teaching me that maybe I always should have thought like this.

Adapt or die, as they say!
